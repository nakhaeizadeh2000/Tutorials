### 4) **Common confusion**

- "Graphs are where advanced algorithms live" — graphs are *connected power*; the advanced mechanics distribute across paradigms (06: DP on DAGs, flows) and modeling (03: implicit search at scale). This domain is the connectivity core, not the whole frontier.
- "Boundaries leaves are filler" — they are the DRY contract made visible: each states what its domain deliberately does *not* teach so the repo can teach it once.
