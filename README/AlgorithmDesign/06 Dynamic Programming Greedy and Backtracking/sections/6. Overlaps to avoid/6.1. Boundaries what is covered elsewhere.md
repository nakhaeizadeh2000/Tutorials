### 4) **Common confusion**

- "Paradigms are where advanced algorithms live" — paradigms are *optimality thinking*; the advanced mechanics distribute across structures (04), graphs (05), and modeling (03). This domain is the decision layer, not the whole stack.
- "Boundaries leaves are filler" — they are the DRY contract made visible: each states what its domain deliberately does *not* teach so the repo can teach it once.

### 5) **Performance note**

No runtime cost to boundaries — the performance is editorial: one-line links instead of copied sections keep every leaf short, searchable, and single-sourced. When Domain 05 changes settling guidance, one domain updates instead of seven.
