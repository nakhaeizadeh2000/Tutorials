**Definition** — bounds, cases, amortization, space analysis, measurement (Domain 01); sorting, searching, selection, lower bounds (Domain 02); pointers, windows, stacks, queues, string matching (Domain 03) — the *tools and techniques* every claim here used — belong to their domains; this domain applies them to structures, never re-derives them. When a leaf says "resize amortizes" or "KMP generalizes to Aho-Corasick," the *method* is a link ([Domain 01 §2.1](<../../../01 Complexity and Algorithmic Thinking/sections/2. String matching without the naive scan.md>)), and only the *application* is new.

- **Why the boundary matters**: analysis re-derived per structure rots independently — five amortization explanations diverging, three average-case framings drifting. Single source of truth: the machinery gets its full treatment once (Domains 01–03), everywhere else one line plus a link. This leaf is the map that keeps those lines short for the whole domain.
- **Modern guidance** — if a passage here needs more than one sentence of notation/case/sort/pointer reasoning, stop and link instead. The one-sentence versions: averages need distributions (01 §1.2), resizes amortize (01 §2.1), queues order breadth (03 §3.1), KMP never rescans (03 §3.2). Everything else here is *structures* wearing those tools.

### 4) **Common confusion**

- "Structures are where advanced algorithms live" — structures are *organized power*; the advanced mechanics distribute across graphs (05) and paradigms (06). This domain is the warehouse, not the whole supply chain.
- "Boundaries leaves are filler" — they are the DRY contract made visible: each states what its domain deliberately does *not* teach so the repo can teach it once.

### 5) **Performance note**

No runtime cost to boundaries — the performance is editorial: one-line links instead of copied sections keep every leaf short, searchable, and single-sourced. When [Domain 05](<../../../05 Graphs/README.md>) changes frontier guidance, one domain updates instead of seven.
