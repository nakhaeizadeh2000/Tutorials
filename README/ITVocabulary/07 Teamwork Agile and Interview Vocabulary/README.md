## 1. Rhythm words

### [1.1. Sprint, backlog, and velocity](<./sections/1. Rhythm words/1.1. Sprint backlog and velocity.md>)

1. **Sprint is a time-boxed bet** (fixed scope, fixed length — planning becomes commitment, not forecasting).
2. **Backlog is ordered options** (prioritized, estimated, refined — the queue strategy, not a wishlist).
3. **Velocity measures capacity, not worth** (completed work per sprint — forecasting fuel, never a performance score).

### [1.2. Standup, retro, and demo as process](<./sections/1. Rhythm words/1.2. Standup retro and demo as process.md>)

1. **Ceremonies serve the cadence** (each ritual protects one thing: sync, improvement, feedback — cut any that protects nothing).
2. **Retro improves the system** (process fixes with owners — the sprint's compound interest).
3. **Demo closes the loop** (working software in front of stakeholders — feedback while change is cheap).

## 2. People words

### [2.1. Roles, stakeholders, and ownership](<./sections/2. People words/2.1. Roles stakeholders and ownership.md>)

1. **Roles name accountabilities** (PM, designer, engineer, EM — who decides what, explicitly).
2. **Stakeholder means affected-and-vocal** (anyone with skin in the outcome — map them before they surprise you).
3. **Ownership is DRI, not tenancy** (directly responsible individual — one neck per problem, even in shared code).

### [2.2. Feedback, ladder, and one-on-ones](<./sections/2. People words/2.2. Feedback ladder and one-on-ones.md>)

1. **Feedback is specific and soon** (behavior + impact + request — the format that changes things).
2. **Ladder calibrates growth** (levels, scope, expectations — promotion as legible criteria, not lottery).
3. **One-on-ones are yours, not theirs** (the report's meeting — career, blockers, context — agenda from below).

## 4. Important points to remember (teamwork)

### [4.1. Teamwork vocabulary checklist (mental models mentors insist on)](<./sections/4. Important points to remember/4.1. Teamwork vocabulary checklist mental models mentors insist on.md>)

1. **Name the cadence** (sprint length, ceremony purpose — in every process complaint).
2. **Name the owner** (DRI per problem — before any escalation).
3. **Name the growth ask** (level, scope gap, timeline — in every career conversation).
