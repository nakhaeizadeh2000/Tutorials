**Definition** — this domain owns delivery *use* (inspecting services, profiling with action, configuring, shipping); *tool mechanics* live in the JavaScript track and are assumed, never re-taught. Debugger stepping, breakpoints, and console toolkit → [JavaScript 15/5.1](<../../../../JavaScript/15 Error Handling and Debugging/sections/5. Debugging toolkit/5.1. Heap snapshots, allocation timelines, and GC internals → [JavaScript 18/5.1](<../../../../JavaScript/18 Memory Management and GC/sections/5. Measuring and preventing leaks/5.1. - **What problem it solves**: double-teaching (stepping re-explained per service leaf — copies drifting; GC generations restated per profile — contradictions accumulating). Single source of truth keeps every copy fresh (one definition, many links — updates land once). Readers navigate faster too (breakpoint confusion routes to JS 15 in one click — no delivery leaf wastes pages re-deriving stepping).
- **Modern guidance** — when a question reduces to tooling, link out in one line (never re-derive — "stepping: [JS 15/5.1]" then continue with the service point). When a JS-track reader arrives here, the prerequisites (domain index §0) tell them what's assumed — no surprise dependencies. Propose tooling-side corrections upstream (errors in JS 15/18 fixed there — never patched by local restatement).

### 2) **Runtime and packaging**

**Definition** — this domain owns *delivery* (observing, configuring, shipping running services); the *runtime* and *arrival* live in Domains 01–02 and are assumed. Loop, phases, and process lifecycle (what inspectors pause, what exits number) → [Domain 01](<../../../01 Runtime Fundamentals and Mental Model/README.md>) (delivery assumes execution — never re-derives it). Manifests, lockfiles, and installs (what images freeze, what `ci` reproduces) → [Domain 02](<../../../02 Packages Scripts and Project Layout/README.md>) (delivery assumes packaging — never re-derives it). Runtime and packaging questions route in one line.

- **Why it matters (maintainability)**: the delivery/runtime/packaging split prevents misplaced depth (phases re-derived per profile leaf — stale within a release; lockfiles re-justified per image — two copies, one rots). Each side links the other (slow drains rooted in loop behavior route precisely — "phases: [01/3.1]" — instead of guessing). Boundaries make all three sides shorter and more accurate.
- **Modern guidance** — deliver here, execute and arrive there (a delivery question about *phases* routes to 01; about *locks* routes to 02; about *rollouts* stays). Quote the boundary in reviews ("delivery-side" vs "runtime-side" vs "packaging-side" — different venues, different owners). Keep this domain's runtime coverage consumer-shaped (lag probes, exit codes, frozen trees — assumed tools; theory — 01/02).

### 3) **Synthesis capstone**

**Definition** — this domain delivers the pieces (observed, configured, shippable services); *production judgment across all six* lives in [Domain 07](<../../../07 Production Node Use and Interview Mastery/README.md>) (the track's capstone: selecting architectures under constraints, reviewing Node code, narrating systems in interviews). Delivery questions stay here; synthesis questions wait one domain. The capstone assumes 01–06 fluency (runtime vocabulary + packaging discipline + data-path fluency + conversation mechanics + parallelism judgment + delivery rigor — the complete backend engineer).

- **Why it matters (progression)**: pieces-before-synthesis ordering (judgment without mechanics is opinion — capstone earns its weight on six domains of receipts; mechanics without synthesis is trivia — 07 assembles the engineer). Skipping ahead borrows confusion at interest; the map makes the capstone cheap.
- **Modern guidance** — read in `NN` order (on-disk prefixes are curriculum order — 06 before 07, always). Treat the textual forward as promise, not placeholder (names its owning domain — when 07 lands, links upgrade; until then, the name scopes the question). Record cross-domain fixes at the boundary (a delivery bug found while synthesizing gets fixed here and linked — never duplicated into 07).
