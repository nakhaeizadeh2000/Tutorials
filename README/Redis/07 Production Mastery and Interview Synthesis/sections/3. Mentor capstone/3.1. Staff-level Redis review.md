## 2. Decisions carry prices (every choice windowed — trade-offs explicit, never vibed)

**Definition** — priced decisions: staff judgment quantifying every trade ([Domain 02 §1.1 windows](<../../../02 Caching Patterns at Scale/sections/1. Write patterns/1.1. **What problem it solves** — the unpriced-decision trio: *vibes deciding* (feelings choosing — numbers missing (trade-offs hiding — costs externaling (judgment performing — rigor absenting (fashion ruling — evidence missing); *prices separating* (staleness stating sans coupling (decisions halving — wholes fragmenting (trade-offs partialing — honesty halving (judgment splitting — wisdom dividing); *costs discovering* (incidents pricing — outages billing (surprises charging — budgets blowing (numbers absenting — planning guessing (arithmetic missing — fiction billing). **Modern guidance** — the pricing lines per decision (review-enforced — capstone, never repetition): *staleness numbering* (TTL bounding — freshness costing (bounds stating — rot declining (decisions half-pricing — vibes halving); *durability numbering* (fsync choosing — windows stating (safety costing — throughput spending (policies matching — honesty earning (decisions halving — vibes ending); *availability numbering* (coupling stating — outages bounding (uptime spending — resilience designing (trade-offs owning — judgment proving (decisions completing — vibes absenting); *wholes pricing* (layers summing — totals bounding (numbers completing — vibes declining (trade-offs owning — judgment proving).

**Mentor note** — teach "no number, no decision" (mentees quantifying per choice design priced judgment on first watching; vibed decisions interrogated ("priced how?" — numbers demanding per decision). **Definition** — judgment multiplication: staff wisdom transferring via teaching (checklists instructing (01–06 §4.1s guiding — reviews structuring (novices deciding — quality stabilizing (knowledge encoding — teams scaling); drills coaching (game-days facilitating — scenarios adapting (participants deciding — judgment exercising (practice guiding — readiness building (experience transferring — panic inoculating); mastery compounding (mentees mentoring — wisdom cascading (judgment multiplying — organizations learning (knowledge compounding — heroes retiring (buses surviving). **What problem it solves** — the hoarded-mastery trio: *heroes bottlenecking* (experts deciding — teams waiting (judgment centralizing — buses endangering (knowledge hoarding — organizations fragiling (scaling capping — velocity gating); *reviews gatekeeping* (verdicts ruling — reasoning hiding (mentees obeying — judgment unbuilding (wisdom concealing — growth stunting (teaching missing — multiplying absenting); *drills commanding* (scenarios dictating — participants executing (decisions removing — judgment bypassing (practice commanding — readiness feigning (coaching missing — experience untransferring). **Modern guidance** — the multiplication lines per mentor (review-enforced — capstone, never repetition): *checklists teaching* (01–06 guiding — reviews structuring (novices deciding — quality stabilizing (knowledge encoding — teams scaling (wisdom transferring — judgment building); *drills coaching* (scenarios facilitating — participants deciding (judgment exercising — readiness building (experience transferring — panic inoculating (coaching guiding — commanding declining); *mentees mentoring* (wisdom cascading — judgment multiplying (organizations learning — knowledge compounding (heroes retiring — buses surviving (mastery compounding — hoarding ending); *thinking narrating* (traversals voicing — decisions explaining (reasoning showing — judgment modeling (wisdom demonstrating — mastery teaching).
