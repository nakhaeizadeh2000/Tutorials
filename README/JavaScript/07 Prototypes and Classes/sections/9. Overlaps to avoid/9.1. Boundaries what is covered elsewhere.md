## 9.1. Boundaries: what is covered elsewhere

### 1) **Property descriptors, Object.* utilities, and immutability — Objects in Depth (06)**

`value`/`writable`/`enumerable`/`configurable`, `defineProperty`/`getOwnPropertyDescriptor`/`getOwnPropertyDescriptors`, `Object.assign`/`keys`/`entries`/`Reflect.ownKeys` enumeration order, and `preventExtensions`/`seal`/`freeze` shallow semantics are owned by [Objects in Depth](../../../06%20Objects%20in%20Depth/README.md) ([descriptors](../../../06%20Objects%20in%20Depth/sections/2.%20Properties%20and%20descriptors/2.1.%20Data%20vs%20accessor%20descriptors%20and%20attribute%20defaults.md>), [defining](../../../06%20Objects%20in%20Depth/sections/2.%20Properties%20and%20descriptors/2.2.%20Defining%20observing%20and%20configuring%20properties.md>), [enumerability](../../../06%20Objects%20in%20Depth/sections/2.%20Properties%20and%20descriptors/2.3.%20Enumerability%20enumeration%20order%20and%20for-in%20mechanics.md>), [utilities](../../../06%20Objects%20in%20Depth/sections/4.%20Object%20utilities%20and%20copying/4.1.%20Assign%20merging%20shallow%20copy%20and%20fromEntries.md>), [seal/freeze](../../../06%20Objects%20in%20Depth/sections/5.%20Integrity%20and%20immutability/5.1.%20Prevent%20extensions%20seal%20freeze%20and%20shallow%20immutability.md>)). This domain links there for descriptor shapes and does not re-derive them — it focuses on *which* prototype object descriptors live on.

### 2) **this binding rules, arrow lexical this, and call/apply/bind — Functions Deep Dive (05)**

Call-site rules (`default`/`implicit`/`explicit`/`new`), arrow capture, `call`/`apply`/`bind` permanent `this`, and `this` loss in callbacks/timers are owned by [Functions Deep Dive](../../../05%20Functions%20Deep%20Dive/README.md) ([call-site rules](../../../05%20Functions%20Deep%20Dive/sections/3.%20this%20binding/3.1.%20Call-site%20rules%20default%20implicit%20explicit%20new%20and%20strict%20mode.md>), [arrow](../../../05%20Functions%20Deep%20Dive/sections/3.%20this%20binding/3.2.%20Arrow%20lexical%20this%20and%20why%20callbacks%20break%20or%20heal.md>), [bind](../../../05%20Functions%20Deep%20Dive/sections/3.%20this%20binding/3.3.%20call%20apply%20bind%20and%20bound%20functions.md)). This domain treats `this` as the delegation receiver but does not re-teach binding — it assumes that model and applies it to prototype methods.

### 3) **Declaration scope, hoisting, TDZ, and closure capture — Variables, Scope, and Closures (04)**

Lexical scope chains, `let`/`const`/`var` contracts, TDZ, `CreatePerIterationEnvironment`, and module vs script top-level are owned by [Variables](../../../04%20Variables%20Scope%20and%20Closures/README.md) ([TDZ](../../../04%20Variables%20Scope%20and%20Closures/sections/1.%20Declarations%20and%20bindings/1.2.%20Temporal%20dead%20zone%20why%20early%20access%20throws.md>), [hoisting](../../../04%20Variables%20Scope%20and%20Closures/sections/2.%20Scope%20and%20lookup/2.2.%20Hoisting%20the%20reality%20creation%20phase%20not%20magic.md>), [closures](../../../04%20Variables%20Scope%20and%20Closures/sections/3.%20Closures/3.1.%20Closures%20functions%20that%20remember%20their%20birth%20environment.md>)). Class TDZ and `#field` lexical scope in this domain mirror that terminology but do not re-derive it.

### 4) **Collections, WeakRef/FinalizationRegistry, and identity — Collections / Memory (09, planned 18)**

`Map`/`Set`/`WeakMap`/`WeakSet`/`WeakRef`/`FinalizationRegistry`, object-identity keying vs stringification for `Object` dictionaries, and GC/heap implications of retained prototype graphs are owned by [Collections](../../../09%20Collections%20Map%20Set%20and%20Weak%20References/README.md) (domain 09) and the upcoming Memory domain (textual forward ref for 18). This domain notes `Map`-vs-object trade-offs on `null`-proto dictionaries but does not teach `Map` APIs or lifetime (see [Map](../../../09%20Collections%20Map%20Set%20and%20Weak%20References/sections/1.%20Map%20deep%20dive/1.1.%20Map%20creation%20SameValueZero%20insertion%20order%20and%20iteration.md) and [WeakMap](../../../09%20Collections%20Map%20Set%20and%20Weak%20References/sections/3.%20Weak%20collections/3.1.%20WeakMap%20ephemeron%20keys%20non-iterability%20and%20use%20cases.md>)).

### 5) **Iterables, generators, async, and JSON/modules — upcoming data and async domains**

`Symbol.iterator`/`Symbol.asyncIterator`, `for-of`, generator `function*`/`yield`, `async`/`await`/`Promise`, `structuredClone`, `JSON` replacer/reviver, and `import`/`export` live bindings belong to upcoming domains (textual refs to 10/13/14/16). Class async methods and generator methods (`*method()`, `async method()`) are mentioned as existing shapes in [3.1](../3.%20Class%20syntax/3.1.%20Class%20basics%20declarations%20hoisting%20methods%20accessors%20and%20fields.md) but not taught as protocol.

### 6) **Stop-list for this domain (what not to add here)**

- Do not duplicate descriptor definition, `defineProperty` mechanics, or `Object.keys` vs `Reflect.ownKeys` partition — link to 06.
- Do not re-derive `this` call-site binding or arrow capture — link to 05.
- Do not re-derive scope TDZ or closure per-iteration environment — link to 04.
- Do not teach `Map`/`WeakMap` APIs, iterator protocol, or `Promise` combinators — link to 09/10/13.
- Do not teach `JSON` stringify/parse or module import/export — link to 14/16.
