# 6.1. Boundaries what is covered elsewhere

## 1. Intuition lives in Domain 01 (entities, keys, cardinality — the questions this domain sharpens)

**Definition** — inbound boundary: Domain 01 ([Modeling Foundations and Mental Model](<../../../01 Modeling Foundations and Mental Model/README.md>)) teaches *what to ask* (what exists, how it relates, what must stay true); this domain teaches the *formal sharpening* (which dependency shapes are legal where). The dependency is one-way and intentional: intuition first, formalism second — arrows before numbers, method before nomenclature.

**What problem it solves** — formalism without intuition produces designers who can name 3NF and cannot spot a transitive chain in a real schema; intuition without formalism produces designers who split correctly but cannot defend the split under challenge ("why is this better?" deserves "transitive dependency eliminated," not "feels cleaner"). The two domains compose: Domain 01 graduates model by habit, Domain 02 graduates prove and price by rule. Neither alone is the complete skill.

**Modern guidance** — the crossing map backward: entities and lifecycles → [Domain 01 §1](<../../../01 Modeling Foundations and Mental Model/sections/1. What modeling means/1.2. Entities relationships and cardinality.md>); key strategy (surrogate default, candidate keys) → [Domain 01 §2](<../../../01 Modeling Foundations and Mental Model/sections/2. Identity and keys/2.1. Surrogate versus natural keys.md>); nouns-verbs-constraints method → [Domain 01 §3](<../../../01 Modeling Foundations and Mental Model/sections/3. First design moves/3.1. From requirements to tables.md>); review and convention habits → [Domain 01 §4](<../../../01 Modeling Foundations and Mental Model/sections/4. Mentor checklist/4.1. Modeling checklist mentors insist on.md>). Readers arriving here without Domain 01 should detour for §1–§3 at minimum — dependencies assume entities, normal forms assume keys.

**Common confusion** — *"I know normal forms — can I skip Domain 01?"* (Asked and answered in [Domain 01 §6](<../../../01 Modeling Foundations and Mental Model/sections/6. Boundaries/6.1. Boundaries what is covered elsewhere.md>), restated here per the boundary-leaf contract: definitions are not the skill.) If the whiteboard exercise in [5.1](<../5. Interview QA/5.1. Common interview QA normalization.md>) feels effortless — arrows, anomaly, split, stop — skip freely. Otherwise the intuition work pays first, and pays more than another pass over definitions.

**Performance note** — none specific — this domain's performance content (write amplification removed, join shapes priced, refresh policies) stands on its own; Domain 01's performance contribution (modeling mistakes as the costliest bottlenecks) is its complement. Joined: shape correctly (01), sharpen formally (02), operate cheaply (both).

**Mentor note** — diagnose by failure direction (same habit as Domain 01 §6): recites forms but models poorly → send back to Domain 01; models well but can't defend splits → keep here, drill narration. Prescribe by direction, not seniority — and run the combined drill (whiteboard a schema *and* defend its normal form) as the graduation test for both domains together.

**Cross-links** — the map above is the complete inbound set; the remaining promises cover forward and outward boundaries.

## 2. Documents bend these rules on purpose (Domain 03 owns embed-versus-normalize for Mongo)

**Definition** — forward boundary: Domain 03 (Document Modeling for Scale — unbuilt) owns where normalization *deliberately stops* for document databases: embedding (denormalized by design, one document per aggregate) versus referencing (normalized across collections), with the access-pattern rules that decide. This domain's forms govern relational truth; Domain 03 governs document shape — related disciplines, different optimizers (query patterns, not dependency purity).

**What problem it solves** — applying 3NF to Mongo produces reference-spaghetti (a dozen collections joined in application code, the worst of both worlds); applying embedding instincts to Postgres produces blobs and arrays where constraints should be (Domain 02 §1.2's BAD examples). Each store has its own normal: relational normal is dependency-pure, document normal is access-shaped. The boundary keeps both normals intact — learn each where it lives, translate deliberately, never by habit.

**Modern guidance** — until Domain 03 lands: relational designs from this domain transfer to Mongo only as *starting decompositions* (the entities and dependencies are real in both worlds), never as final shapes (the splits re-merge along access patterns). The transfer rule in one sentence: dependencies discovered here inform what *can* split; access patterns (Domain 03) decide what *should*. No document modeling lives in this domain beyond that sentence — per DRY, it waits for its home.

- Domain 03 (forward — unbuilt): aggregate boundaries, embed-vs-reference rules, schema patterns (subset, computed, bucket), migration between shapes.
- Domains 04–07 (forward — unbuilt, full list in [LOG.md](<../../../LOG.md>)): relationships at scale, evolution, operations modeling, production mastery.

**Common confusion** — *"denormalization (3.1) is just embedding."* Cousins, not twins: denormalization copies relational truth for measured hot paths (truth stays queryable, copies rebuildable); embedding *is* the truth's shape in documents (no separate normalized source behind it). Different guarantees, different failure modes, different reviews — 3.1's six-field record does not transfer unchanged. Learn embedding in Domain 03; don't extrapolate from copies.

**Performance note** — the performance stories differ by store and stay separated: relational copies cost refresh/write-amplification (3.1); document embeds cost document growth and unbounded arrays (Domain 03). Conflating them misprices both — another reason each lives in its own domain.

**Mentor note** — when learners ask "but how in Mongo?" from inside this domain, answer with the transfer sentence plus the forward pointer — then note *why* the answer waits: premature document advice, given before access-pattern thinking exists, becomes "embed everything" cargo-cult. Sequencing is pedagogy: dependencies first (here), access second (03), translation last.

**Cross-links** — document-shape mechanics (the engine side) in Databases 03 (next promise); deliberate-denormalization discipline whose habits transfer in [3.1](<../3. Judgment/3.1. Deliberate denormalization with priced trade-offs.md>).

## 3. Mechanics live in Databases (constraint syntax, migration execution — linked, not repeated)

**Definition** — outward boundary: how the engine *executes* what this domain *decides*. Normal forms name the legal shapes; Databases-track mechanics implement them: constraint DDL variants, index types and EXPLAIN, migration tooling and zero-downtime execution, isolation for the races constraints can't close. Neither track repeats the other — links cross per PROMPT.md §5.

**What problem it solves** — duplicated coverage rots (two FK-syntax explanations drifting apart), and misplaced coverage confuses (migration *execution* taught beside normal *forms* suggests they're one decision — they're two: what shape, then how to get there safely). Single source of truth means each concept has one home; this leaf maps the crossings so readers never wonder and writers never paste.

**Modern guidance** — the crossing map, by need: constraint/index DDL and migration authoring → [Databases 02](<../../../../Databases/02 Postgres Deep Dive/README.md>); document-shape mechanics → [Databases 03](<../../../../Databases/03 MongoDB Deep Dive/README.md>); transactions, locking, race-safe enforcement → [Databases 05](<../../../../Databases/05 Transactions Consistency and Integrity/README.md>); EXPLAIN, index selection, contention measurement → [Databases 06](<../../../../Databases/06 Performance Backup and Operations/README.md>); production operation and interview synthesis → [Databases 07](<../../../../Databases/07 Production Data Use and Interview Mastery/README.md>); full orientation → [Databases track index](<../../../../Databases/README.md>). Inbound, Databases 6.1 boundary leaves point here for normalization depth — each track owning its side.

**Common confusion** — *"this track assumes SQL — where do I learn it?"* (Asked in Domain 01 §6; restated for arrivals landing here directly.) Databases 01–02, in order, then return. The tracks compose: Databases makes you operational, Database Design makes you deliberate — either order works, operational-first is the common path.

**Performance note** — none specific — performance mechanics (EXPLAIN, index selection, refresh monitoring) are Databases 02/05/06 territory by design. This domain's performance contribution is upstream: dependency-pure shapes with priced, recorded copies cost less to operate than shapes that cannot state their own guarantees.

**Mentor note** — same navigation discipline as Domain 01 §6: mechanics questions get links, not lessons — then the *why* of the boundary. Learners who navigate the map become autonomous; mentors who re-teach across boundaries train dependency. The boundary is curriculum, not convenience.

**Cross-links** — the map above is the complete outbound set; repository/ORM consumption boundaries (unchanged from Domain 01) in [Domain 01 §6](<../../../01 Modeling Foundations and Mental Model/sections/6. Boundaries/6.1. Boundaries what is covered elsewhere.md>) — linked, not repeated.
