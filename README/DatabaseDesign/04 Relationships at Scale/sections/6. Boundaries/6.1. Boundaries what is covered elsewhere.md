# 6.1. Boundaries what is covered elsewhere

## 1. Cardinality basics live in Domain 01 (the foundation this domain loads)

**Definition** — inbound boundary: Domain 01 ([Modeling Foundations](<../../../01 Modeling Foundations and Mental Model/README.md>)) teaches relationships as constraint choices (one-to-many as FK + NOT NULL, many-to-many as junction, cardinality as priced bets — [Domain 01 §1](<../../../01 Modeling Foundations and Mental Model/sections/1. What modeling means/1.2. Entities relationships and cardinality.md>)), plus the both-ways review habit ([Domain 01 §4](<../../../01 Modeling Foundations and Mental Model/sections/4. Mentor checklist/4.1. Modeling checklist mentors insist on.md>)) and key strategy ([Domain 01 §2](<../../../01 Modeling Foundations and Mental Model/sections/2. Identity and keys/2.1. Surrogate versus natural keys.md>)). Domain 04 loads that foundation and applies volume: cardinality bets meet traffic, junctions meet growth, keys meet skew. No cardinality concept is re-taught here — only its behavior under load, which is genuinely new.

**What problem it solves** — foundation-skipping (modeling junctions at scale without the both-ways habit — single-direction indexes, unnarrated traversals — or debating surrogate-vs-natural per junction instead of inheriting the team default). Scale decisions assume foundation fluency: "index both directions" assumes the reviewer knows both directions *exist* (01 §1); "name the celebrity" assumes cardinality bets were priced at design (01 §1's third promise). Readers arriving here without Domain 01 should detour for §1–§2 at minimum — volume multiplies unexamined foundations into structural incidents.

**Modern guidance** — the crossing map backward: cardinality and junction basics → [Domain 01 §1](<../../../01 Modeling Foundations and Mental Model/sections/1. What modeling means/1.2. Entities relationships and cardinality.md>); both-ways narration → [Domain 01 §4](<../../../01 Modeling Foundations and Mental Model/sections/4. Mentor checklist/4.1. Modeling checklist mentors insist on.md>); key defaults and candidate keys → [Domain 01 §2](<../../../01 Modeling Foundations and Mental Model/sections/2. Identity and keys/2.1. Surrogate versus natural keys.md>); nouns-verbs method (where junctions are first spotted) → [Domain 01 §3](<../../../01 Modeling Foundations and Mental Model/sections/3. First design moves/3.1. From requirements to tables.md>). Junction dependency purity (composite keys, 2NF placement) → [Domain 02 §2.1](<../../../02 Normalization Deep Dive/sections/2. The core ladder/2.1. Second and third normal form.md>); copy-pricing ledgers (counter caches, precomputed counts) → [Domain 02 §3.1](<../../../02 Normalization Deep Dive/sections/3. Judgment/3.1. Deliberate denormalization with priced trade-offs.md>).

**Common confusion** — *"scale changes the rules, so foundations don't apply."* Scale changes *prices*, never *rules*: cardinality still constrains (bets just cost more when wrong), junctions still need both directions (unnarrated ones just fail louder), dependencies still place attributes (misplaced ones just anomaly at volume). Foundations are load-invariant; volume reveals violations faster and punishes them harder. "We move fast" never exempts cardinality — it raises the price of skipping it.

**Performance note** — none specific — this domain's performance content (index discipline, traversal economics, skew math, fan-out rules) is self-contained; Domain 01's contribution (modeling mistakes as costliest bottlenecks) and Domain 02's (write amplification removed, join shapes priced) compose underneath. Three layers, one ledger: shape correctly (01), sharpen formally (02), load for volume (04).

**Mentor note** — diagnose by failure direction (track-wide habit, fourth domain): single-direction junctions → missing both-ways habit, send to 01 §4; unpriced cardinality → missing bets, send to 01 §1; misplaced junction attributes → missing dependency lens, send to 02 §2.1; volume failures with sound foundations → keep here, drill skew/fan-out. Prescribe by direction, not seniority — and run the combined drill (model a social feature end-to-end: entities, forms, junctions, celebrities) as the four-domain graduation test.

**Cross-links** — the map above is the complete inbound set; forward and outward boundaries follow.

## 2. Junction mechanics live in Domains 01–02 (the rules scale inherits)

**Definition** — forward-compatible boundary (plus one forward pointer): composite-key identity, 2NF placement, and junction-as-hidden-entity spotting are owned by Domains 01–02 and *inherited* here, not repeated. Domain 04 adds only what volume contributes: reverse indexes, attribute growth with promotion triggers, skew audits, fan-out rules, mitigation toolkits. The division is crisp — correctness rules there, volume behavior here — and every leaf in §2–§3 links back to the inherited rule before adding the scale layer.

**What problem it solves** — rule duplication rot (two "junction composite key" explanations drifting apart — one recommending surrogate PKs for junctions, the other composite — readers caught between) and scale reinventing correctness (skew mitigations designed for junctions whose dependency placement was never checked — fast anomalies, still anomalies). Inheritance keeps one rule with many applications: 01 §1 says junctions exist, 02 §2.1 says attributes sit on full keys, 04 §2.1 says both directions get indexed and celebrities get named. One lineage, three layers, zero contradiction.

**Modern guidance** — the inheritance map (rule → scale application): junction existence and composite identity (01 §1) → bidirectional indexing + growth room (04 §2.1); 2NF attribute placement (02 §2.1) → promotion triggers to entity-hood (04 §2.1); hidden entities in verbs (01 §3) → larval-entity foresight (04 §2.1); copy ledgers (02 §3.1) → counter caches and precomputed counts (04 §3.1); fan-out diagnosis (02 §2.2's 4NF) → split-query rule (04 §3.1). Forward: partitioning and sharding keys for hot junctions (hash by pair, colocate celebrities deliberately) belong to Domain 05 (forward — unbuilt); cross-shard FK unenforceability (1.1's narrow exception) resolves there too. Full curriculum in [LOG.md](<../../../LOG.md>).

**Common confusion** — *"this domain repeats junctions from Domain 01."* (Anticipated and answered by structure: 01 §1 teaches *existence* (junctions as constraint choice, one example); 04 §2.1 teaches *volume behavior* (reverse indexes, growth, skew — six new rules, zero repeated definitions). Overlap is by reference ("composite PK (01 §1)"), never by restatement. Readers feeling déjà vu should check: the familiar sentence is always a link, the new content always adjacent. Familiarity orients; novelty teaches — the DRY contract working as designed.

**Performance note** — none specific beyond §2–§3's own economics — the inheritance point *is* performance-relevant though: correctly-placed attributes (02) make covering indexes possible (04's INCLUDE clauses index real attributes, not misplaced ones); honest arcs (04 §2.2) make join planning possible (planner trusts FKs it can see). Correctness layers enable performance layers — misplaced foundations cap what volume work can achieve. Order matters: 01, then 02, then 04 — the track sequence is a dependency graph, not a suggestion.

**Mentor note** — teach inheritance explicitly ("this rule comes from 01 §1 — we apply it at volume"): juniors seeing rules *reused* across domains learn the track as one system, not four courses; juniors seeing each domain "start over" memorize four disconnected checklists. Cross-domain citations in reviews ("per 02 §2.1 placement, this attribute stays; per 04 §2.1 growth, we add the promotion trigger") model the integrated thinking seniors are hired for. Cite lineage; think in systems.

## 3. Locking and index mechanics live in Databases (engine behavior — linked, not repeated)

**Definition** — outward boundary: how the engine *executes* what this domain *decides*. FK lock behavior (what locks, how long, what queues), B-tree internals (why composite leading columns matter, how INCLUDE covers, when skip-scans help), CONCURRENTLY builds (non-blocking index creation under traffic), keyset-vs-OFFSET planner behavior, replica lag semantics — all engine mechanics owned by the Databases track. Domain 04 names the decision (index the FK, birth-index junctions, paginate by keyset, route celebrities); Databases teaches the machinery underneath.

**Modern guidance** — the crossing map, by need: constraint/index DDL, `CONCURRENTLY` builds, and the unindexed-FK audit query → [Databases 02](<../../../../Databases/02 Postgres Deep Dive/README.md>); transactions, lock queues, and long-transaction behavior (CASCADE blast radius, purge batching) → [Databases 05](<../../../../Databases/05 Transactions Consistency and Integrity/README.md>); EXPLAIN, index selection, planner behavior (covering indexes, skip-scans) → [Databases 06](<../../../../Databases/06 Performance Backup and Operations/README.md>); keyset-vs-OFFSET pagination mechanics → [Databases 07 §2.2](<../../../../Databases/07 Production Data Use and Interview Mastery/sections/2. Review and design/2.2. Data API contracts.md>); production operation, replica routing, and lag monitoring → [Databases 06–07](<../../../../Databases/06 Performance Backup and Operations/README.md>); full orientation → [Databases track index](<../../../../Databases/README.md>). Inbound, Databases boundary leaves defer relationship-scale depth here — each track owning its side.

**Common confusion** — *"this track assumes SQL and indexes — where do I learn them?"* (Asked in every boundary leaf; restated for direct arrivals landing here.) Databases 01–02 for SQL and constraints, 05–06 for transactions and performance — in order, then return. The tracks compose: Databases makes you operational, Database Design makes you deliberate — operational-first is the common path. Either order works; skipping either half doesn't.

**Performance note** — none specific — performance mechanics (lock monitoring, EXPLAIN reading, lag alerting) are Databases 02/05/06 territory by design. This domain's performance contribution is upstream: decision rules (index every FK, both junction directions, keysets over OFFSET, celebrities named with triggers) that make the mechanics' job possible — unindexed shapes can't be tuned, only remodeled. Decide correctly here; tune successfully there.

**Mentor note** — same navigation discipline as every boundary leaf in this track (fourth restatement, permanent value): mechanics questions get links, not lessons — then the *why* of the boundary. Learners who navigate the map become autonomous; mentors who re-teach across boundaries train dependency. The boundary is curriculum, not convenience.
