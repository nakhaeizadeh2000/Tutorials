# 4.1. Mentor checklist for the whole track

## 1. Teaching order is the track order (intuition before formalism, relational before document)

**Definition** — the pedagogical contract: teach domains in track order (01 intuition → 02 formalism → 03 documents → 04 volume → 05 evolution → 06 operations → 07 synthesis) because each layer assumes the last (dependencies need keys named; junctions need forms audited; capacity needs widths measured; outboxes need aggregates bounded; synthesis needs all six lived). Reordering breaks prerequisites silently (normal forms before entities — definitions without referents; documents before dependencies — shapes without anomaly vocabulary; operations before evolution — gauges without triggers). Sequence is pedagogy, not bureaucracy — the dependency graph made calendrical.

**What problem it solves** — the two sequencing failures: *formalism-first* (normal forms week one — juniors memorizing 3NF without entities to apply it to — definitions without referents, recall without derivation — the knowledge that evaporates by week four); and *interest-first* (documents first "because Mongo is fun" — shapes without dependency lenses — unbounded nests designed confidently, anomalies discovered painfully — enthusiasm preceding discipline). Both produce brittle knowledge (recitable, inapplicable — interviews exposing the gap within minutes: "normalize this" met with definitions, "scale this" met with fashion). Ordered teaching produces layered competence (each layer practiced on the previous layer's outputs — handoffs learned as method, not just documented as process).

**Modern guidance** — the teaching calendar (per learner or cohort, paced by mastery not weeks): **01** until entities/keys/cardinality fluent (whiteboard bookstore unaided — graduation test below); **02** until dependencies/arrows/anomalies narrated (normalize-on-demand — arrows before verdicts, reflexively); **03** until access-first habitual (catalogs before shapes — screens sketched, vetoes honored); **04** until volume-priced (FKs indexed, junctions bidirectional, celebrities named — scale vocabulary with numbers); **05** until change-phased (expand/contract narrated, capacity computed, triggers set — futures budgeted); **06** until operations-shaped (audit scoped, outbox surfaced, gauges owned — reliability designed); **07** capstone + interviews (method performed whole — graduation). Gate advancement per domain (tests next promise — demonstrable, never self-assessed); revisit earlier layers when later layers wobble (volume confusion → re-drill 01 cardinality — foundations, not remediation-shame).

```sql
-- GOOD: the ordered curriculum — prerequisites chained, gates enforced (excerpt).
-- Week 1-2: 01 (entities/keys/cardinality — graduate: whiteboard bookstore + defend keys).
-- Week 3-4: 02 (dependencies/forms — graduate: normalize live + price a copy).
-- Week 5-6: 03 (documents/access — graduate: catalog-then-shape exercise + veto story).
-- Week 7-8: 04 (volume — graduate: junction both-ways + celebrity mitigation priced).
-- Week 9-10: 05 (evolution — graduate: phased migration + capacity math live).
-- Week 11-12: 06 (operations — graduate: outbox surface + gauges owned).
-- Week 13: 07 (capstone + interviews — graduate: traveling answer + incident parables).
-- Gates enforced (demonstrable per domain); wobbles recurse (later confusion → earlier drill).
```

```sql
-- BAD: the shuffled curriculum — prerequisites assumed, foundations missing.
-- Week 1: normal forms (definitions without entities — memorized, inapplicable).
-- Week 2: documents (shapes without dependencies — nests unbounded, anomalies pending).
-- Week 3: interviews (traveling answers without lenses visited — vocabulary parade).
-- Operations/volume/evolution: skipped ("advanced" — deferred forever — seniors designing
-- flag-days with unindexed FKs, unphased migrations, ungauged relays). Order shuffled;
-- prerequisites voided; competence fragmented. Fast calendar, hollow graduates.
```

**Common confusion** — *"experienced engineers can start anywhere (skip 01–02)."* Experience self-assessed misleads (seniors "knowing normalization" who can't draw dependency arrows live — recall without derivation, exposed by 02's graduation test in minutes). Placement by demonstration (graduation tests per domain — test out by performing, never by asserting seniority — staff engineers whiteboarding bookstores gladly, juniors attempting fearlessly). Test-out honors experience (fast passage for fluent performers — no condescension, pure demonstration); self-placement honors confidence (uncorrelated with competence — Dunning-Kruger scheduled as curriculum). Demonstrate, don't assert — placement by performance, all levels, no exceptions.

**Performance note** — ordered-teaching economics: prerequisites chained (each layer practiced on previous outputs — learning efficiency maximized — handoffs learned as method); gates enforced (demonstrable advancement — no hollow graduates consuming senior review cycles re-teaching foundations); wobbles recursed (later confusion → earlier drill — targeted remediation, not course repetition — coaching precise, time bounded). Shuffled teaching costs re-teaching (foundations retaught per advanced confusion — mentor-hours burning); gated ordering costs tests (minutes per gate — cheapest assessment available). Order once; gate always; recurse precisely.

**Mentor note** — protect sequence against impatience (learners rushing to documents/interviews ("boring basics" — skipping to fun parts); managers compressing calendars ("two-week modeling course" — seven domains in ten days — fragments guaranteed)). Impatience accommodated via test-outs (fluent performers advance fast — demonstrated, never asserted — speed earned, not granted); impatience indulged via skipping produces hollow graduates (fast calendar, failing interviews, incidents scheduled). Pace by mastery (gates enforced), accelerate by demonstration (test-outs honored), never by assertion (confidence uncalibrated). Sequence protected; mastery gated; graduates genuine.

## 2. Drills per domain, capstones across (timed narrations + integrated exercises)

**Definition** — the two drill tempos (mirroring review cadence from [1.2](<../1. Track synthesis/1.2. Design review as a practice.md>)): **per-domain drills** (timed narrations — arrows/catalogs/prices/triggers performed live per domain §5.1 — minutes each, weekly during that domain — fluency built); **cross-domain capstones** (integrated exercises — one feature through all lenses — quarterly or per-milestone — composition built). Drills train moves (derivation reflexes — arrows drawn, catalogs listed, prices computed — automatic under time pressure); capstones train performance (whole method under observation — layers sequenced, transitions narrated, handoffs honored). Moves without performance fragment (brilliant drills, uncomposed features); performance without moves collapses (capstones attempted without drill fluency — slow, silent, ungraded).

**What problem it solves** — the two training failures: *drill-only fluency* (timed arrows perfected — capstone never attempted — moves uncomposed (features modeled single-layered despite drill mastery — transfer absent — practice-theoretic, production-fragmented)); and *capstone-only ambition* (integrated exercises assigned without drill foundations — slow silent struggles (no arrows to draw, no catalogs to list — capstones attempted vocabulary-first, failing comprehensively, discouraging deeply). Drills-then-capstones sequences both (moves automatic before performance demanded — fluency preceding composition, confidence built on competence). Training like athletics (drills daily, games weekly — moves before matches, always); unlike academia (lectures then finals — knowledge tested unperformed — the failure mode this track exists to prevent).

**Modern guidance** — the drill calendar (per domain during its weeks, capstones at milestones): *drills* (each domain §5.1's timed narrations — 90-second verdicts, 5-minute whiteboards, 3-minute estimations — rehearsed weekly — times recorded (fluency measurable — rehearsal times dropping = readiness rising)); *capstones* (one feature, seven lenses — [1.1](<../1. Track synthesis/1.1. The modeling method end to end.md>)'s procedure — quarterly per team, monthly per learner in intensive programs — reviewed layer-by-layer (gaps located precisely — targeted drill follow-ups prescribed)); *progression* (drill times gate capstone readiness (fluent moves → attempt performance); capstone gaps gate drill focus (wobbled layer → drill that domain — targeted, never generic "study more")). Fluency measured (times), composition witnessed (reviews), gaps prescribed (drills) — training engineered, not hoped.

```sql
-- GOOD: training engineered — drills timed, capstones witnessed, gaps prescribed (excerpt).
-- Drills (weekly per domain): arrows 90s (02), catalog 3min (03), verdicts 90s rapid-fire (04),
-- estimation 3min live math (05), claim-query writing 90s (06) — times recorded, improving.
-- Capstones (quarterly): paid-orders feature, seven lenses, reviewed layer-by-layer —
-- gaps located ("forms solid, skew unexamined") → prescribed (drill 04/5.1 × 3 rounds).
-- Progression: drill fluency → capstone attempt → gap prescription → drill focus → re-attempt.
```

```sql
-- BAD: training hoped — lectures attended, finals feared, transfer absent.
-- Lectures (seven domains presented — attendance recorded, comprehension unmeasured).
-- Finals (capstone assigned cold — no drill foundations — slow silent struggles graded
-- harshly — discouragement, not diagnosis). Gaps undescribed ("study modeling more" —
-- generic, unactionable — learners re-reading randomly, improving accidentally).
-- Drills absent (moves unpracticed — arrows undrawn under pressure); capstones rare
-- (composition unattempted — fragments uncomposed). Attended thoroughly; learned thinly.
```

**Common confusion** — *"drills are interview prep, not engineering training."* Drills train derivation reflexes (arrows/catalogs/prices/triggers under time pressure — the same reflexes production incidents demand (outage diagnosis timed, migration decisions pressured, launch reviews hurried — speed with method, always)). Interview performance is a *side effect* of operational reflexes (candidates performing method briskly under interview pressure perform it identically under incident pressure — interviewers selecting for production traits via timed exercises, correctly). Train reflexes for production (incidents, launches, reviews — timed realities); collect interviews as bonus (same reflexes, friendlier stakes). Drills serve on-call first, hiring second — both well.

**Performance note** — training economics: drills cost minutes weekly (timed narrations — cheapest fluency available — rehearsal times as readiness metrics, free); capstones cost hours quarterly (integrated exercises — diagnosis complete — gaps located precisely, coaching targeted); unpracticed incidents cost quarters (derivation under pressure without reflexes — slow, silent, wrong — production grading the untrained). Drill minutes prevent incident quarters — the steepest training ROI in engineering (minutes-to-quarters ratio exceeding 1000× on first prevented repeat — stated finally, track complete).

**Mentor note** — run drills visibly (mentors performing timed narrations first — authority demonstrating fluency, not just demanding it — "watch me arrows in 90 seconds" teaches more than "practice arrows"). Time learners kindly (records, not rankings — fluency personal, improving — never leaderboards (competition poisons practice — anxiety replacing fluency — drill culture dies with first ranking). Witness capstones thoroughly (layer-by-layer reviews — gaps prescribed precisely — coaching targeted, morale preserved (gaps normal, prescription clear, progress visible). Demonstrate, time kindly, witness thoroughly, prescribe precisely — mentorship in four verbs, track complete.

## 3. Graduation tests gate advancement (demonstrable, never self-assessed)

**Definition** — the gates: per-domain graduation demonstrations (01: whiteboard bookstore + defend keys; 02: normalize live + price a copy; 03: catalog-then-shape + veto story; 04: junction both-ways + celebrity mitigation priced; 05: phased migration + capacity math live; 06: outbox surface + gauges owned; 07: traveling answer + incident parables) — performed live before a mentor (or recorded for review — async acceptable, unwitnessed unacceptable), judged pass/retry (retry with prescribed drills — never fail-and-abandon — gaps coachable, always). Self-assessment ("I know normalization") gates nothing (confidence uncalibrated — Dunning-Kruger scheduled as curriculum without gates); demonstration gates everything (performed method — witnessed, judged, recorded).

**What problem it solves** — the two advancement failures: *time-served advancement* (weeks completed → next domain unlocked — attendance mistaken for competence — hollow graduates accumulating (fragments per domain, composition never — advancing calendar, stagnant capability)); and *confidence-based advancement* ("I got it" → skip drills → next domain — self-assessment uncalibrated (overconfident advancing hollow; underconfident stalling despite fluency — both miscalibrated, both ungated). Gates convert advancement to evidence (demonstrated method per domain — witnessed fluency, recorded progression); retry converts failure to prescription (gaps located → drills assigned → re-attempt scheduled — failure as diagnosis, never verdict). Advancement earned visibly; retry coached precisely; competence compounded genuinely.

**Modern guidance** — the gate contract per domain (demonstration + judge + retry): *demonstrate* (domain's graduation test performed live — timed per §5.1 prescriptions — transitions narrated, prices computed, artifacts shown — complete performance, not fragments); *judge* (mentor passes or prescribes (pass: advance; retry: gap located + drills assigned + re-attempt dated — binary with prescription, never vague "needs work")); *record* (pass logged with date + evidence link (recording/transcript — institutional memory of competence — staffing decisions informed (who's fluent where — project assignment by demonstrated capability, not claimed)); *retry kindly* (gaps normal — prescription clear — re-attempt scheduled within two weeks — momentum preserved, shame absent — retry culture (failing forward visibly) beats pass culture (advancing hollowly)). Gates enforced (unskippable — even staff engineers demonstrate (gladly — fluency performs fast)); retries bounded (three attempts → coaching conversation (method mismatch? motivation? — humane inquiry, never bureaucratic loop)).

```sql
-- GOOD: gates evidenced — demonstrations witnessed, retries prescribed, competence recorded.
-- 01 gate: whiteboard bookstore + key defense (passed 2026-10-14 — recording linked).
-- 02 gate: live normalization + copy pricing (retry 1: arrows undrawn → drill 02/1.1 ×3 →
-- retry 2 passed — gap closed measurably). 04 gate: junction + celebrity priced (passed).
-- Staffing: project X needs volume fluency → assignees 04-passed (capability-matched).
-- Retries: kind, bounded, prescriptive (failure as diagnosis — culture learning, never shaming).
```

```sql
-- BAD: advancement assumed — attendance logged, competence hoped.
-- Week 4 complete → 03 unlocked (02 graduation: unattempted — arrows undrawn, anomalies
-- unnarrated — advancing hollow). "I know joins" → 04 skipped (junction directions single —
-- admin screens scanning within quarters). Senior joins → placed advanced (asserted fluency —
-- untested — mentoring juniors in fragments while missing forms personally). Retries: none
-- (failure unacknowledged — gaps invisible — hollow compounding). Attendance itemized;
-- competence absent; advancement automatic. Graduates certified; capabilities fictional.
```

**Common confusion** — *"gates slow down fast learners."* Gates *accelerate* fluent performers (demonstration takes minutes for the fluent — test-outs honored immediately — speed earned visibly) while *protecting* teams from hollow advancement (ungated confidence advancing gaps that consume senior review cycles re-teaching foundations — quarters of drag per hollow graduate). Fast learners love gates (showcase fluency, advance rapidly — recognition); hollow advancers fear them (exposure — correctly, kindly, with prescription). Gate speed correlates with fluency (fast passes for the fluent — the system rewarding competence with velocity). Slow down the hollow; speed up the fluent — gates do both simultaneously, automatically.

**Performance note** — gate economics close the track's training argument finally: gates cost minutes per demonstration (mentor time — the scarcest teaching resource, spent precisely on witnessed performance); ungated advancement costs quarters (hollow graduates consuming reviews, scheduling incidents, requiring remediation — expensive, diffuse, unbounded). Retry prescriptions cost drill rounds (targeted, bounded — gaps closing measurably); unprescribed failure costs abandonment (learners stalling silently — talent lost, morale damaged). Minutes of witnessing prevent quarters of hollowness — assessment ROI exceeding all other teaching investments (witnessed competence compounds; assumed competence decays). Gate always; retry kindly; record permanently.

**Mentor note** — celebrate passes publicly (graduation acknowledged — team visibility for demonstrated fluency — recognition motivating more than mandates ever could); normalize retries privately-kindly (retry framed as prescription ("arrows need drills — three rounds, re-attempt Friday" — diagnosis + plan + date — failure without shame, progress without doubt). Never weaponize gates (advancement withheld punitively — gates as control — culture poisoned instantly, permanently); always wield educationally (gates as diagnosis — competence mapped, coaching targeted — culture learning, durably). Celebrate passes; normalize retries; wield educationally — mentorship's closing verbs, track complete.
