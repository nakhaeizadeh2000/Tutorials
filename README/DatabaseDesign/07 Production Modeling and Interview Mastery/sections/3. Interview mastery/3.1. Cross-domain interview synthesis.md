# 3.1. Cross-domain interview synthesis

## 1. Follow the question across domains (one answer, four lenses)

**Definition** — the synthesis skill: interview questions answered by *traveling* domains visibly — "how would you store orders?" answered through 01 (entities, keys, FK sentences) → 02 (3NF audit, computed totals) → 04 (FKs indexed, skew asked, fan-out noted) → 05 (capacity row, email migration phased) → 06 (outbox, audit mirror, gauges) — each lens applied briefly, transitions narrated ("forms clean — now volume..."), depth allocated by question emphasis (schema questions dwell 01–02, scale questions dwell 04–05, reliability questions dwell 06). Single-lens answers pass junior loops; traveling answers pass senior+ loops (breadth with prices, method visible throughout).

**What problem it solves** — the excellent-fragment interview (candidate brilliant on normalization, silent on skew, blank on outbox — depth without breadth — hired as specialist, rejected for senior-generalist scope); and the shallow-tour interview (all seven domains touched in ninety seconds each — breadth without depth — vocabulary parade, no prices anywhere). Traveling answers balance dynamically (dwell where probed — follow interviewer emphasis; touch all lightly — show range; price everywhere — numbers per lens). Interviewers probe depth by dwelling (follow-up questions deepening one lens — answered deeply, then re-broadened unprompted — "deeper on skew? gladly — then back to evolution..."). Depth on demand, breadth by default — the senior interview shape.

**Modern guidance** — the traveling method (same skeleton as [Domain 01 §5](<../../../01 Modeling Foundations and Mental Model/sections/5. Interview QA/5.1. Common interview QA modeling foundations.md>)'s whiteboard, extended track-wide): (1) *entities first, always* (2 min — nouns, keys, FK sentences — 01 performed, foundations visible); (2) *forms second* (2 min — dependencies, anomalies eliminated, copies priced-or-absent — 02 performed, rigor visible); (3) *volume third* (3 min — FK indexes, junction directions, celebrities named, fan-out split — 04 performed, scale visible); (4) *change fourth* (2 min — capacity row, migration phases, partition/archive triggers — 05 performed, futures visible); (5) *operations close* (2 min — outbox, audit, gauges with owners — 06 performed, reliability visible); (6) *documents where fit* (1 min — aggregates-or-not stated with reason — 03 acknowledged, non-choice documented). Transitions narrated ("forms clean — volume now..."), prices per lens (numbers everywhere), stopping reasoned (time-boxed breadth, offered depth — "happy to go deeper anywhere").

```sql
-- GOOD (traveling answer, orders schema — 12 min narrated, lenses traced):
-- "Entities: customers/orders/lines, surrogate PKs, email UNIQUE (01). Forms: city with
-- customer (3NF); totals computed (no stored copy — read path assembles). Volume: FKs
-- indexed both lenses; no junctions (1-N stated); no skew surface (no social — asked);
-- reporting splits lines×events (fan-out ruled out structurally). Change: 50k/d, 420B
-- rows, triggers set (partition 80M, archive 13mo). Operations: outbox (relay surface),
-- audit mirror on money, gauges owned (lag, depth). Documents: N/A (transactional access —
-- documented non-choice). Deeper anywhere? (offered — depth on demand, breadth delivered)."
```

```sql
-- BAD (single-lens brilliance — depth without travel):
-- "Third normal form throughout — city depends on customer_id transitively, so split;
-- BCNF holds (no overlapping keys); no partial dependencies (surrogate PKs)..."
-- (Volume? Unasked — FKs bare. Change? Unasked — flag-day migrations assumed. Operations?
-- Unasked — dual-write incidents scheduled. Documents? Unmentioned — non-choice undocumented.)
-- Brilliant 02, silent 01/03/04/05/06. Depth admired; breadth absent; senior scope unproven.
-- Or the mirror: seven domains in seven sentences (tour without prices — vocabulary parade).
```

**Common confusion** — *"senior interviews want deep expertise in one area."* Staff/senior loops want *T-shaped* depth (one lens deep — dwell where probed, genuine expertise demonstrated — plus all lenses traveled — range proven, prices everywhere). Pure-I candidates (single-lens brilliant) hire as specialists; pure-dash candidates (tour without prices) hire nowhere senior. Travel default, dwell on demand — T performed live (broad base narrated briskly, deep stem extended on follow-ups, re-broadened unprompted after). T-shape performed, not claimed — interviewers grade the shape, not the assertion.

**Performance note** — traveling answers carry per-lens prices (same habit as every QA leaf in this track, seventh and final domain: FK lookups microseconds, junction traversals indexed, capacity percentages, relay lag gauges — numbers per lens, arithmetic throughout). Prices prove production contact (unpriced breadth is tourism — visited lenses, lived in none); per-lens numbers prove residence (lived experience per domain — incidents survived, ledgers kept). Tourists describe; residents price — interviewers distinguishing via numbers, always. Price every lens visited; dwell where numbers run deepest.

**Mentor note** — drill traveling answers with phase timing (entities 2min, forms 2min, volume 3min, change 2min, operations 2min, documents 1min — timed segments, transitions narrated — same phase-timing prescription as all QA leaves). Grade travel completeness ("which lens skipped?" — skipped lenses fail the exercise regardless of covered brilliance) and transition narration ("forms clean — volume now" — transitions audible, method visible). Timed travel drills convert fragment-experts to traveling seniors in weeks — the final interview transformation, track complete.

## 2. System-design adjacent answers (capacity, evolution, operations in schema interviews)

**Definition** — the adjacency skill: schema interview answers extended — briefly, priced — into system-design territory (capacity math beside table shapes; migration phases beside DDL; outbox/gauges beside entities; partition keys beside growth curves). Adjacency demonstrates scope (schema decisions with systems consequences — stated, priced, owned) without derailing (schema remains the subject — adjacent topics visited (30–60 seconds each), never occupied — depth offered ("happy to go deeper on partitioning"), breadth delivered). Pure-schema answers pass schema loops; adjacent answers pass system-design-adjacent loops (staff+ scope — schemas in systems context, always).

**What problem it solves** — the schema-in-vacuum interview (tables designed without capacity (500M-row implications unmentioned — "and at scale?" asked late, answered vaguely); without evolution (flag-day migrations assumed — "how do you ship this live?" met with silence); without operations (dual-write notifications proposed — honesty architecture absent). Vacuum answers hire implementers (correct shapes, unexamined consequences); adjacent answers hire owners (shapes with systems envelopes — capacity/evolution/operations priced per shape). Staff loops probe envelopes deliberately ("and the migration?" "and at 10×?" "and the notifications?" — three adjacencies, each expected); vacuum candidates fail all three; adjacent candidates collect all three as scoring opportunities.

**Modern guidance** — the adjacency habit (three envelopes per schema answer, 30–60 seconds each): *capacity envelope* ("50k/d × 420B × 18mo ≈ 33GB — 7% budget — trigger partition at 80M" — [Domain 05 §2.1](<../../../05 Evolution Growth and Partitioning/sections/2. Growth and capacity/2.1. Capacity modeling with measured triggers.md>) compressed); *evolution envelope* ("email column ships expand/contract — nullable, backfill chunked, contract witnessed — zero-downtime by phases" — [Domain 05 §1.1](<../../../05 Evolution Growth and Partitioning/sections/1. Schema evolution/1.1. Expand and contract migrations.md>) compressed); *operations envelope* ("notifications via outbox (relay surface, purge windowed); money audited (mirror); gauges owned (lag, depth)" — [Domain 06](<../../../06 Modeling for Operations/README.md>) compressed). Envelopes offered, not imposed (schema first — envelopes after the shape lands — depth on invitation ("deeper on any envelope?")). Adjacent breadth, schema depth — proportions honored, scope proven.

```sql
-- GOOD (schema answer with envelopes — shapes plus systems, priced):
-- "Schema: customers/orders/lines (surrogate PKs, email UNIQUE, FKs indexed both lenses).
-- Envelopes: capacity 33GB/18mo (7% — partition trigger 80M set); evolution phased
-- (email expand/contract — backfill chunked, contract witnessed); operations outboxed
-- (relay surface + purge windowed, audit mirror on money, gauges owned). Documents N/A
-- (transactional — stated). Deeper on any envelope? (offered — depth available, breadth delivered.)"
```

```sql
-- BAD (vacuum schema — correct tables, unexamined systems):
-- "Customers, orders, lines — PKs, FKs, done." (Capacity? Unmentioned — 500M-row future
-- unpriced. Evolution? Unmentioned — flag-day assumed. Operations? Unmentioned —
-- dual-write proposed when asked, honesty absent. Documents? Unmentioned — non-choice
-- undocumented.) Correct shapes, vacuum-sealed. Staff loop asks three envelope questions;
-- vacuum candidate answers vaguely thrice. Shapes hired junior; envelopes hire staff.
```

**Common confusion** — *"adjacency derails schema interviews (stay on topic)."* Envelopes *are* the topic at staff scope (schemas live in systems — capacity/evolution/operations inseparable from shapes that survive). Derailing means occupying adjacent topics (10-minute partitioning lecture uninvited — depth misplaced); adjacency means visiting briefly (30–60 seconds per envelope — breadth demonstrated, depth offered). Visit, don't occupy — offer, don't impose — envelope, don't derail. Interviewers inviting deeper ("tell me more about the migration") reward the visit with extended scoring; interviewers moving on bank the breadth silently. Both outcomes favorable — adjacency never penalized when proportioned.

**Performance note** — envelope economics in interviews (same pattern as every QA leaf: numbers prove residence — capacity percentages, migration chunk sizes, relay lag gauges — arithmetic per envelope, 30 seconds each). Unenveloped schemas read as unoperated (designed, never run — the junior tell at staff loops); enveloped schemas read as operated (run at scale, migrated live, paged on gauges — the staff tell). Operated-ness performed via envelopes — three numbers (capacity %, migration chunks, relay lag) proving production contact in ninety seconds total. Numbers hire; shapes qualify; envelopes promote.

**Mentor note** — teach envelopes as "and..." habit (juniors finishing schema answers add "and capacity... and evolution... and operations..." — three ands, thirty seconds each — practiced until reflexive). The "and" reflex generalizes beyond interviews (design docs enveloped, PR descriptions enveloped, incident reviews enveloped — completeness as habit, not performance). Drill "and" thrice per whiteboard exercise (schema, then three ands — timed, graded) — reflexive completeness within weeks. And capacity, and evolution, and operations — always, forever, hired.

## 3. Narrate method, not recall (arrows, catalogs, prices, triggers — the performance that hires)

**Definition** — the meta-skill closing the track's interview arc (stated per-domain from 01 through 06, synthesized here finally): interviews grade *method performed* (dependencies drawn live — arrows before verdicts; catalogs listed — workload before shapes; prices stated — numbers before claims; triggers named — runbooks before hopes) over *knowledge recalled* (definitions recited, forms named, patterns listed — vocabulary without derivation). Method narrated out loud is unmistakable and unfakeable (interviewers hear decisions happening — arrows drawn, then splits; catalogs listed, then shapes; prices computed, then verdicts); recall recited is equally unmistakable (definitions first, application absent — "3NF means..." followed by silence on the schema at hand).

**What problem it solves** — the recall trap (candidates studying definitions (normal forms memorized, pattern names listed, acronyms fluent) while neglecting derivation (arrows undrawn, catalogs unlisted, prices uncomputed — vocabulary without method). Recall answers interview *questions* ("define 3NF" — recited); method answers interview *exercises* ("normalize this" — derived live — the exercise form senior loops use exclusively). Recall-heavy preparation optimizes the wrong test (junior phone screens quiz definitions; senior loops stage exercises — preparation mismatched to loop level fails upward-bound candidates systematically). Method preparation (timed derivations — arrows/catalogs/prices/triggers performed live, repeatedly) optimizes the exercised form — hired at every level, promoted at senior ones.

**Modern guidance** — the method performance (four moves, every exercise, narrated aloud): *arrows* ("customer_id → city — transitive through non-key — split" — dependencies drawn before verdicts — [Domain 02 §1.1](<../../../02 Normalization Deep Dive/sections/1. Dependencies and atomicity/1.1. Functional dependencies the one idea behind every normal form.md>)); *catalogs* ("800k/day order-screen, realtime — embed lines; 50k/day history — subset" — workload before shapes — [Domain 03 §1.2](<../../../03 Document Modeling for Scale/sections/1. Aggregates and access/1.2. Access patterns drive document shape.md>)); *prices* ("both directions indexed at birth; flagship capped with cached counts — 04/2.1 + 04/3.1 ledgers referenced" — numbers before claims — [Domain 04](<../../../04 Relationships at Scale/README.md>)); *triggers* ("partition at 80M (platform); archive past 13mo (data) — runbooks, not hopes" — [Domain 05 §2.1](<../../../05 Evolution Growth and Partitioning/sections/2. Growth and capacity/2.1. Capacity modeling with measured triggers.md>)). Moves performed in order (arrows → catalogs → prices → triggers — the track's sequence compressed to method), narrated continuously (silence ungradeable — thinking aloud, always).

```sql
-- GOOD (method performed — arrows, catalogs, prices, triggers, narrated continuously):
-- "Arrows first: order_id → customer_id → city (transitive — split city with customer).
-- Catalog: order-screen 800k/d realtime (embed lines, one fetch); history 50k/d (subset).
-- Prices: FKs indexed both lenses (microseconds per write); flagship capped + counts cached
-- (celebrity budgeted). Triggers: partition 80M (platform), archive 13mo (data), relay lag
-- 5min (gauges owned). Method visible throughout — arrows drawn, then splits; catalog
-- listed, then shapes; prices computed, then verdicts; triggers named, then calm."
```

```sql
-- BAD (recall recited — vocabulary without derivation, silence where method belongs):
-- "Third normal form eliminates transitive dependencies. Denormalization trades integrity
-- for performance. Partitioning improves query performance on large tables. Outbox pattern
-- ensures reliable messaging." (Arrows? Undrawn — no dependencies on the schema at hand.
-- Catalog? Unlisted — no workload named. Prices? Uncomputed — no numbers anywhere.
-- Triggers? Unnamed — hopes unvoiced. Four definitions, zero derivations. Vocabulary
-- parade, method absent. Recited, not performed. Ungradeable beyond junior recall.)
```

**Common confusion** — *"method narration feels slow (just give the answer)."* Narration speed follows fluency (practiced method narrates briskly — arrows in seconds, catalogs in a minute — timed drills from every §5.1 compressing performance to interview pace); silent "answers" arrive ungradeable (verdict without derivation — correct-or-not coin flip, no partial credit path, no follow-up surface). Interviewers score derivation steps (arrows drawn: points; catalog listed: points; prices computed: points — partial credit per move); silent verdicts score binary (right/wrong — no recovery from wrong). Narrated method maximizes expected score (partial credit per move + follow-up surface per step); silent answers minimize it (binary, fragile). Narrate briskly; score continuously.

**Performance note** — method performance *is* interview performance (same pattern as every QA leaf, seventh and final synthesis): timed drills compressing arrows/catalogs/prices/triggers to interview pace (minutes per move — fluency measurable in rehearsal times); phase timing per exercise (entities 2min, forms 2min, volume 3min, change 2min, operations 2min — budgets honored live); transition narration (audible method — "forms clean — volume now" — interviewers tracking progress, never wondering). Rehearse timed; perform briskly; transition audibly — interview mechanics mastered like query mechanics (measured, drilled, owned).

**Mentor note** — teach method-first preparation (juniors drilling derivations (timed arrows/catalogs/prices/triggers) over definitions (flashcards, glossaries) — preparation matched to exercised loops, not quizzed ones). The preparation audit ("show me your last timed derivation" — rehearsal evidence, not study hours) diagnoses readiness precisely (fluent method = ready; fluent definitions = junior-loop ready only). Preparation prescribing derivations hires seniors; prescribing definitions hires juniors. Prescribe derivations; time them; perform them; hired — the track's pedagogy performing whole, mentors equipped, track complete.
