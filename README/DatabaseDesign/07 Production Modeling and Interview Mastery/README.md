## 1. Track synthesis

### [1.1. The modeling method end to end](<./sections/1. Track synthesis/1.1. The modeling method end to end.md>)

1. **Seven domains, one sequence** (requirements → entities → dependencies → documents → relationships → evolution → operations — each layer assuming the last)
2. **The worked capstone** (one feature modeled through all seven lenses — the method performed whole)
3. **Handoff discipline between layers** (each domain's output is the next domain's input — named explicitly)

---

### [1.2. Design review as a practice](<./sections/1. Track synthesis/1.2. Design review as a practice.md>)

1. **One review ritual for all domains** (boundaries, catalogs, prices, blessings — the unified template)
2. **Registers are the institutional memory** (decisions, blessings, triggers, versions — four ledgers, one habit)
3. **Review cadence by lifecycle** (per-PR checks, quarterly audits, yearly strategy — rhythm, not heroics)

## 2. Production incidents

### [2.1. Classic modeling incidents and their fixes](<./sections/2. Production incidents/2.1. Classic modeling incidents and their fixes.md>)

1. **The orphan storm** (dropped FKs meeting buggy writers — traced to Domains 01 and 04)
2. **The ceiling collision** (unbounded nests meeting physics — traced to Domains 02 and 03)
3. **The fiction dashboard** (fan-out sums meeting finance — traced to Domains 02 and 04)

---

### [2.2. From incidents to prevention](<./sections/2. Production incidents/2.2. From incidents to prevention.md>)

1. **Postmortems name missing artifacts** (which checklist, record, or trigger would have caught it — blameless, precise)
2. **Prevention is scheduled** (triggers, audits, and drills from every incident — never "be careful")
3. **Incident stories teach** (the story bank habit — three war stories per domain, told to every junior)

## 3. Interview mastery

### [3.1. Cross-domain interview synthesis](<./sections/3. Interview mastery/3.1. Cross-domain interview synthesis.md>)

## 4. Mentor checklist

### [4.1. Mentor checklist for the whole track](<./sections/4. Mentor checklist/4.1. Mentor checklist for the whole track.md>)

1. **Teaching order is the track order** (intuition before formalism, relational before document — sequence is pedagogy)

3. **Graduation tests gate advancement** (whiteboard + defend + price per domain — demonstrable, not self-assessed)
