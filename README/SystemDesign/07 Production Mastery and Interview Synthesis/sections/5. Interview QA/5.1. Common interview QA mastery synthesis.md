**Definition** — the systems answer: candidates composing full designs under brief pressure (designs answering — scopes clarifying (loads estimating — honesty reviewing (answering proving — blobbing declining); trade-offs defending — options pricing (futures stressing — fashions declining (defending funding — vibing declining); evidence composing — chains proving (depths embedding — audits sizing (composing owning — claiming declining).

**What problem it solves (by answering)** — weak systems answers recur because candidates tour boxes (components listing — arrows drawing (technologies naming — mastery claiming (touring billing — judgment failing); compose numbers instead (scopes clarifying — binders naming (stores choosing — gaps closing (composing funding — touring declining); price trade-offs (options numbering — futures multiplying (hypes refusing — surprises ending (pricing owning — vibing declining). The answer entry converts tours to chains ("designs compose — see [1.1](<../1. Full-system design/1.1. Full-system designs answering briefs.md>) briefs plus chains plus depths (scopes clarified, binders named, stores chosen — blobs ended); trade-offs price — see [1.2](<../1. Full-system design/1.2. Full-system trade-offs defending choices.md>) options plus futures plus declined fashions (winners justified, cliffs dated, hypes refused). Interviewers grading numbers hire designers; tour guides fail regardless of boxes drawn.

**Modern guidance** — the answering lines per systems question (review-enforced): *designs diagnosing* ([1.1](<../1. Full-system design/1.1. Full-system designs answering briefs.md>) — scopes clarifying (loads estimating — honesty reviewing (diagnosing proving — touring refusing (diagnoses earning — blobs ending); *trade-offs diagnosing* ([1.2](<../1. Full-system design/1.2. **Common confusion** — *"systems answers test memorized designs (recitals passing)."* Passing reciting known systems (URLs shortening — chats messaging (diagrams reproduced — numbers missing (passing charging — judgment failing); answering composing live (briefs answering — chains composing (trade-offs defending — gaps closing (answering earning — reciting declining). **Performance note** — answering economics: diagnosing costing rows (scopes clarifying — binders naming (options pricing — gaps closing); touring equivalents costing fails (boxes toured — numbers missing (judgment unproven — offers withheld). Diagnose cheaply (systems — rows); tour never (interviews — boxes).

**Mentor note** — teach "answer every systems question with briefs, chains, and priced trade-offs" (juniors diagnosing one systems answer per three rows design tour-proof interviewing on first hearing; box tours interrogated ("users? binder? priced alternative?" — diagnosing demanding per answer). Diagnose one answer once (designs stating — trade-offs defending tangible (touring ending — diagnosing proving permanently).
