## 1. Every domain answers three questions (what/why/cost — judgments complete, never partial)

**Definition** — the completeness habit: what defining mechanics, why justifying existence, cost pricing trade-offs — judged per domain before calling it done (what defining (mechanics stating — behaviors describing (definitions owning — ambiguity declining (clarity serving — juniors funding); why justifying (problems naming — histories tracing (purposes proving — alternatives declining (reason owning — fashion failing (wisdom serving — mastery funding); cost pricing (trade-offs ranging — budgets stating (margins checking — regrets declining (honesty owning — surprises ending (rigor proving — trust earning). Domains answering (what defining — why justifying (cost pricing — judgments completing).

**What problem it solves** — the incomplete trio: *whats missing* (mechanics unstated — behaviors undescribed (defining absenting — ambiguity owning (stating failing — clarity missing (describing lacking — juniors guessing (owning absenting — mastery doubting (funding missing — trust failing); *whys skipping* (problems unnamed — histories untraced (justifying absenting — purposes unproven (naming failing — fashion ruling (tracing lacking — wisdom missing (proving absenting — alternatives unconsidered (declining missing — regret scheduling); *costs hiding* (trade-offs unstated — budgets unpriced (pricing absenting — surprises scheduling (ranging missing — honesty failing (stating lacking — regrets owning (checking absenting — rigor doubting (ending missing — trust eroding). Answering three converting missing to defining (mechanics stating — problems naming (trade-offs ranging — judgments completing); reviewers checking what/why/cost per domain, not page counts (answering proving → verifiable; missing → returned) — synthesizing the full track (Domains 01–06 each deliver all three).

**Modern guidance** — the completeness lines per review (review-enforced): *what defining* (mechanics stating — behaviors describing (clarity owning — ambiguity declining); *why justifying* (problems naming — histories tracing (reason owning — fashion failing); *cost pricing* (trade-offs ranging — budgets stating (honesty owning — surprises ending).

// Zero domains with mechanics-only or praise-only coverage — every leaf ramps junior to mentor (pricing owning — completing proving). Synthesizes Domains 01–06.)

**Common confusion** — *"completeness means length (pages proving)."* Completeness answering questions (what defining — why justifying (cost pricing — judgments completing (rigor owning — partiality declining (mastery serving — trust earning); length padding pages (words multiplying — value missing (bloat owning — reading aching (padding serving — discipline absenting); conflating costing bloated tracks (length admiring — answering missing). "Length" describing pages (words counting — value missing); completeness describing answers (questions owning — judgments completing). Answer three (rigor owning — trust earning); never pad pages (bloating declining — aching ending).

**Performance note** — completeness economics: answering costing three sections (what stating — why tracing (cost ranging — judgments completing (rigor funding — trust earning); missing costing partial judgment (questions unanswered — decisions regretting). Answer cheaply (sections — three); complete always (judgments — domains).

**Mentor note** — teach "what, why, cost — every time" (juniors answering per domain design complete judgment on first hearing; mechanics-only coverage interrogated ("why? cost?" — answering demanding per domain). Answer one domain once (what defining — cost pricing tangible (missing ending — completing holding permanently). Define mechanics; price trade-offs — completeness discipline in three checks, reviewed per domain.
