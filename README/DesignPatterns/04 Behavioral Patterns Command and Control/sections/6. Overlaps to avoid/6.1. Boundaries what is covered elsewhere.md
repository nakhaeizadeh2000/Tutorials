When a leaf says "compose strategies" or "inject observers," the *foundation* is a link ([Domain 01 §3.1](<../../../01 SOLID Principles and Design Foundations/sections/3. Factory Method defer instantiation.md>)), and only the *interaction* is new.

- **Why the boundary matters**: foundations re-derived per behavior rot independently — five composition explanations diverging, three factory framings drifting. Single source of truth: principles/creation/structure get their full treatment once (Domains 01–03), everywhere else one line plus a link. This leaf is the map that keeps those lines short for the whole domain.
- **Modern guidance** — if a passage here needs more than one sentence of SOLID/creational/structural reasoning, stop and link instead. The one-sentence versions: compose by default (01 §3.1), build behind seams (02 §2.2), wrap transparently (03 §2.1). Everything else here is *behavior* wearing those foundations.

### 4) **Common confusion**

- "Behavioral is where advanced patterns live" — behavioral is *interaction*; the advanced mechanics distribute across distribution (05) and modern practice (06). This domain is the conversation core, not the whole language.
- "Boundaries leaves are filler" — they are the DRY contract made visible: each states what its domain deliberately does *not* teach so the repo can teach it once.

### 5) **Performance note**

No runtime cost to boundaries — the performance is editorial: one-line links instead of copied sections keep every leaf short, searchable, and single-sourced. When [Domain 05](<../../../05 Behavioral Patterns Distribution and Interpretation/README.md>) changes Mediator guidance, one domain updates instead of seven.
