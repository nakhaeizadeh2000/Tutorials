### 4) **Common confusion**

- "Distribution is where advanced patterns live" — distribution is *sharing*; the advanced mechanics distribute across modern practice (06) and synthesis (07). This domain is the exchange floor, not the whole market.
- "Boundaries leaves are filler" — they are the DRY contract made visible: each states what its domain deliberately does *not* teach so the repo can teach it once.

### 5) **Performance note**

No runtime cost to boundaries — the performance is editorial: one-line links instead of copied sections keep every leaf short, searchable, and single-sourced. When Domain 06 changes middleware guidance, one domain updates instead of seven.
