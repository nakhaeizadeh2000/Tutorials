Single source of truth: principles and creation get their full treatment once (Domains 01–02), everywhere else one line plus a link. This leaf is the map that keeps those lines short for the whole domain.
- **Modern guidance** — if a passage here needs more than one sentence of SOLID/creational reasoning, stop and link instead. The one-sentence versions: compose by default (01 §3.1), extend without modifying (01 §1.2), build behind seams (02 §2.2). Everything else here is *structure* wearing those foundations.

### 4) **Common confusion**

- "Structural is where advanced patterns live" — structural is *shape-shifting*; the advanced mechanics distribute across behavioral (04–05) and modern practice (06). This domain is the fitting room, not the whole wardrobe.
- "Boundaries leaves are filler" — they are the DRY contract made visible: each states what its domain deliberately does *not* teach so the repo can teach it once.

### 5) **Performance note**

No runtime cost to boundaries — the performance is editorial: one-line links instead of copied sections keep every leaf short, searchable, and single-sourced. When [Domain 04](<../../../04 Behavioral Patterns Command and Control/README.md>) changes Observer guidance, one domain updates instead of seven.
