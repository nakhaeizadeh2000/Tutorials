**Definition** — SRP splitting, OCP seams, LSP contracts, ISP/DIP injection, composition defaults (Domain 01 — all five leaves) — the *principles* every creational decision here applied — belong to Domain 01; this domain instantiates them in creation code, never re-derives them. Foundations checklist habits mentors insist on.md>)), and only the *application* is new.

- **Why the boundary matters**: principles re-derived per pattern rot independently — five OCP explanations diverging, three DIP framings drifting. Single source of truth: foundations get their full treatment once (Domain 01), everywhere else one line plus a link. This leaf is the map that keeps those lines short for the whole domain.
- **Modern guidance** — if a passage here needs more than one sentence of SOLID reasoning, stop and link instead. The one-sentence versions: split by reasons (01 §1.1), extend without modifying (01 §1.2), substitute provably (01 §2.1), inject explicitly (01 §2.2), compose by default (01 §3.1). Everything else here is *creation* wearing those principles.

### 4) **Common confusion**

- "Creational is where advanced patterns live" — creational is *origins*; the advanced mechanics distribute across structural (03), behavioral (04–05), and modern practice (06). This domain is the front door, not the whole building.
- "Boundaries leaves are filler" — they are the DRY contract made visible: each states what its domain deliberately does *not* teach so the repo can teach it once.

### 5) **Performance note**

No runtime cost to boundaries — the performance is editorial: one-line links instead of copied sections keep every leaf short, searchable, and single-sourced. When Domain 04 changes Observer guidance, one domain updates instead of seven.
